My SOC Investigation Workflow

1. Detect: Review alerts and identify suspicious activity.

2. Triage: Validate evidence, severity, scope, and potential impact.

3. Investigate: Correlate logs, accounts, endpoints, IPs, and timestamps.

4. Respond: Recommend containment and escalate according to the playbook.

5. Document: Record evidence, findings, actions, and lessons learned.
