
# Incident Response: Suspected Compromised Account

## Scenario
Simulated alerts indicate unusual authentication activity
on a user account. The scenario is for training purposes.

## 1. Detection and triage
- Validate the alert and relevant timestamps.
- Identify the account, source IPs, and affected systems.
- Check for unusual sign-ins or authentication failures.
- Determine whether the alert is a false positive or needs escalation.

## 2. Investigation
- Correlate identity, endpoint, email, and SIEM events.
- Look for suspicious changes or activity after sign-in.
- Assess the scope and potential business impact.
- Preserve relevant evidence according to policy.

## 3. Containment
Follow the approved incident response process.
Possible actions include revoking sessions, disabling access,
or resetting credentials when justified and authorized.

## 4. Eradication and recovery
- Address the identified cause.
- Restore access securely after validation.
- Monitor for repeated suspicious activity.

## 5. Documentation
Record:
- Incident timeline
- Evidence and indicators
- Affected accounts and assets
- Severity and impact
- Actions and approvals
- Root cause, if established
- Recovery and follow-up tasks

## 6. Lessons learned
Review detection gaps, alert quality, and opportunities
for playbook improvements.

## Important
This is a simulated playbook, not a record of a real incident.
